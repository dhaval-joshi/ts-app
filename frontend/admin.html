Advanced OMS accent color

Used throughout the Advanced OMS section to visually distinguish it from Regular OMS -- controls, tabs, and the whole page background while you're in it.

Portfolio-wide safeguards

One outer daily-loss ceiling across every Advanced OMS Program, on top of Risk Group -- your own stated total risk tolerance for the day, regardless of cause. Strict: crossing it halts every running Program. Optional -- turn off if Risk Group alone covers what you need.

Failures

A centralized, filterable log of operational failures -- broker rejections, price-fetch timeouts, Program orchestration issues -- across both Regular and Advanced OMS.

Broker reconciliation

Checks this app's own records for already-CLOSED live orders against what the broker's own order history actually says happened, catching any drift after the fact. Currently-open positions are never touched here -- that's the live reconcile loop's job, continuously. A Preview run finds and reports discrepancies but corrects nothing; only an explicit Apply run writes anything, and every correction is recorded as a visible amendment on that order's Journal factsheet, never a silent rewrite.
