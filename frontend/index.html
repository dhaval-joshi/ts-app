Strategies

A strategy defines how a position is managed after entry — stop/target as a % or points offset from wherever you actually enter, trailing rules, and when to time-close it. Entry order type, validity, and exit legs are chosen per-trade in New Order.

Identity
Tick size is the instrument's minimum price increment (0.05 for most NSE equity and F&O contracts). Computed SL/Target prices are rounded to the nearest multiple of this, or the broker rejects the order (e.g. "price is not a multiple of tick size"). Check your instrument's actual tick size if it's not the common 0.05.

Time-based close
Timeframe aggregation

Reduces sensitivity to single-tick noise in a choppy market: trailing and the stop/target check re-evaluate on this cadence instead of every raw tick, using the MEDIAN price seen during the window rather than one noisy instant tick. Execution is unchanged -- a confirmed trigger still fires a plain market order. Off (0) = react to every tick, today's exact behavior.

A crossed stop/target must stay crossed for this many evaluations in a row before the close actually fires -- 1 = fire on the first crossing (default). Each evaluation is one raw tick if the interval above is Off, or one window close otherwise.

If the stop is hit and recovers (without closing) this many times, the NEXT hit force-closes immediately -- for a stop that keeps getting tested and bouncing back rather than clearly holding. Stop side only.

Programs

Risk Groups

A correlation-based grouping you define (e.g. "Stock F&O", "Commodity Oil", "Crypto") -- crossing a group's daily-loss cap halts every Program in it, even ones still under their own cap. Sits between Program and Portfolio -- Portfolio-wide safeguards live in Admin.

Sentinel Groups

Macro-programs that automatically rotate active capital across 3 distinct sub-strategies (Sideways, Directional, Volatile) based on the live market regime. Only one sub-strategy trades at a time.

Archived Programs

An archived Program never starts a new cycle, regardless of its status, until unarchived. Any cycle already open at the moment of archiving keeps running normally to close.

Day totals are realized P&L from Advanced OMS cycles closed that day. Click a day to see the orders.

Trading Journal

A durable record of every closed cycle and order -- kept independently of the Program or Strategy that created it, so deleting or editing one later never loses what actually happened. Regular OMS orders that belong to an Advanced OMS Program appear inside their cycle, not listed separately here.

Entry order
Market/stop-market orders automatically include a 5% price-protection band, as Tradejini requires for API orders — nothing to configure.
Sizing
Quantity is estimated as capital ÷ (entry/reference price + 2 point slippage buffer), then rounded down to a whole number of lots — the buffer avoids the order being sized too tight and getting rejected if price ticks slightly before your entry fills.
